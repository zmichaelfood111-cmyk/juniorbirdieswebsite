Who Are We

We are based in Oshawa, serving children ages 7 to 13 from the Oshawa and Whitby area. Our badminton non-profit has been creating an accessible and welcoming space for young players since 2024. Co-founded by high school students Michael and Elaine, our weekly Friday classes are led by the co-founders alongside a dedicated team of volunteers. Our goal is simple: to make badminton accessible to children and help them develop a lasting passion for the sport.
